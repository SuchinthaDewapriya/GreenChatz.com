Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

At GreenChat, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you use GreenChat, we collect the following:

  • Account information: Username, email address (for registered users), age, gender, and country.
  • Messages: Chat messages are stored in our database to enable conversation history. Guest sessions are cleared on logout.
  • Images: Photos shared in chat are stored temporarily. Images with auto-delete timers are permanently removed from our servers after the timer expires.
  • Usage data: We may collect basic usage statistics to improve the service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the GreenChat service
  • To authenticate your account and maintain security
  • To deliver messages to other users in real time
  • To improve our platform and fix bugs

Data Retention

Guest user data and all associated messages are deleted when the user logs out. Registered user data is retained until account deletion is requested. Images with burn timers are deleted from our servers immediately after the timer expires.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including password hashing (bcrypt), JWT authentication tokens, and HTTPS encryption to protect your data.

Third Parties

GreenChat does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not display advertisements.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at privacy@greenchatz.com to exercise these rights.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email us at privacy@greenchatz.com.

About GreenChat

Connecting people since 2025

GreenChatz is a free, real-time chat platform built for people who value both connection and privacy. We believe that online communication should be fast, secure, and accessible to everyone — regardless of location or device.

Our Mission

To build the most secure and user-friendly free chat platform on the internet. We focus on features that matter: instant messaging, private conversations, photo sharing with automatic deletion, and a global community of users from 70+ countries.

What Makes Us Different

  • No ads: GreenChat is completely ad-free. Your conversations are private.
  • Auto-delete photos: Share images that automatically disappear after viewing — like they were never there.
  • Screenshot protection: We alert users when someone takes a screenshot of protected images.
  • Guest access: No email required. Start chatting instantly.
  • Open rooms: Create custom chat rooms with optional password protection.

Technology

GreenChat is built with Node.js, Express, Socket.io for real-time communication, and MongoDB for data storage. Our platform runs securely with JWT authentication and bcrypt password hashing.
